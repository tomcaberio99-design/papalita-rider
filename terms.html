PILOT POLICY

Application, payment and delivery

Application and delivery

Provide accurate contact, identity, and vehicle details as applicable. Before accepting an order, review the pickup and delivery locations, food amount, COD total, delivery fee, and any balance hold. Follow the pickup, cash collection, and return instructions.

Top-ups and reserved balance

Once the approved payment setup is enabled, a confirmed top-up adds to your available balance. Accepting a COD order reserves the food amount. You need enough available balance to accept, and the reservation is released if you are replaced before pickup. The approved provider, top-up and withdrawal rules, and fees will be disclosed before you fund your balance.

After the customer receives the order, collect the full COD amount and record Delivered and COD Collected. Papalita applies the reserved food amount to the store payment. You keep the delivery fee included in the cash collected.

Failed deliveries and review

If you cannot complete the delivery, report the reason and provide the requested evidence of arrival, contact attempts, or return. Verified events outside your control carry a ₱0 wallet charge. Any permitted adjustment follows a review of responsibility and actual loss. You can give your explanation before a decision and request reconsideration afterward.

Delivery earnings and released wallet holds are recorded separately. The final rider terms will explain any compensation for failed attempts and returns, subject to applicable entitlements.

DELIVERY EXCEPTIONS

Failed deliveries and returns

  • Cancelled before pickup. Release the rider’s reservation. Any claim for prepared food follows the store agreement.
  • Customer cancels, refuses, or is unavailable. The rider reports the attempt and follows the return instructions. Release the full hold when the evidence confirms the cause was outside the rider’s control. Verified losses follow the store and Papalita agreement.
  • Store or platform error. Release the rider’s hold after verification. Any loss is settled under the agreement with the responsible party.
  • Possible rider fault. Place the case under review. Papalita hears the rider’s explanation, verifies responsibility and actual loss, and provides a decision before any adjustment allowed by law and the approved terms.
  • Return disputed or unavailable. Record the reason and relevant evidence and refer the case to Papalita support.

Transfers show as paid after provider confirmation. Store, rider, and customer concerns can be reported through Papalita’s published support channel.
